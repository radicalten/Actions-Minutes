Turn your own supported N64 ROM into the 3DS homebrew files, entirely on your computer.

1Select the assets folder

After extracting the ZIP, choose its assets folder. Your browser may call this permission “Upload”; the files stay local.

2Select your ROM

Keep this tab open while building.

Private by design. The builder makes no network requests. The selected assets and ROM are read locally; generated code stays in browser memory until you download the outputs.

3Put the files on your 3DS

    
  1. Copy your original, uncompressed ROM to SD:/3ds/racer/baserom.z64.
  2. Copy the homebrew file to SD:/3ds/Racer3DS.3dsx, or install the CIA with a compatible installer.

    
Use a current desktop browser with WebAssembly and enough free memory. The first compilation can take several minutes. This is experimental; hardware and audio behavior may differ from emulation.
