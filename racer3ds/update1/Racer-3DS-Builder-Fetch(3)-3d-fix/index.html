Racer 3DS Builder

Turn your own supported N64 ROM into 3DS homebrew files. The game build stays on your computer.

1Select the assets folder

After extracting the ZIP, choose its assets folder. Your browser may call this permission “Upload”; the files stay local.

No assets folder selected.

2Select your ROM

Choose the supported US NEPE .z64 ROM, or a ZIP containing it. The exact revision is checked before the build.

Keep this tab open while building.

Private by design. Your ROM and generated game files stay on your computer. This version downloads verified Pyodide and WebAssembly tools from jsDelivr and UNPKG; those services see your IP address and requested tool URLs.

3Put the files on your 3DS

    
  1. Copy your original, uncompressed ROM to SD:/3ds/racer/baserom.z64.
  2. Copy the homebrew file to SD:/3ds/Racer3DS.3dsx, or install the CIA with a compatible installer.

    
An internet connection is needed to fetch the browser tools; the first build may download tens of megabytes. Use a current desktop browser with enough free memory. Compilation can take several minutes. This is experimental; hardware and audio behavior may differ from emulation.
