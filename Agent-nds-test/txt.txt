10-16-26 creating suite.nds based on https://github.com/mattrbeck/mgba-suite-auto/releases/tag/latest and https://github.com/RockPolish/rockwrestler
The goal of this session is to create a test suite for nds, “suite.nds” that works like the mgba test suite “suite.gba”, specifically the automated version found at https://github.com/mattrbeck/mgba-suite-auto/releases/tag/latest The most important thing is full coverage of ARM7 (ARMv4T) and ARM9 (ARMv5TE) supporting both ARM and Thumb instruction sets. A good starting point is the open source rockwrestler.nds test rom https://github.com/RockPolish/rockwrestler which lacks automated functionality and is missing full ARM7/9 coverage and other considerations.
